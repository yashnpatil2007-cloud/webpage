Explore Maharashtra

Discover the beauty, culture and traditions of Maharashtra

Famous Places

Mumbai

Mumbai is known as the city of dreams. It is famous for Marine Drive, Gateway of India and Bollywood.

Lonavala

Lonavala is a beautiful hill station famous for waterfalls, greenery and pleasant weather.

Mahabaleshwar

Mahabaleshwar is popular for strawberries, mountains and amazing viewpoints.

Famous Food

Vada Pav

Vada Pav is the most famous street food of Maharashtra loved by everyone.

Misal Pav

Misal Pav is a spicy and delicious Maharashtrian dish served with pav.

Modak

Modak is a sweet dish specially prepared during Ganesh Festival.

Historic Forts

Raigad Fort

Raigad Fort was the capital fort of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

Sinhagad Fort

Sinhagad Fort is famous for its history and beautiful trekking experience.

Pratapgad Fort

Pratapgad Fort is known for the historic battle of Shivaji Maharaj.
